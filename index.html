Who we are

Give before you take.

Bizcelona is a membership community for Barcelona founders, independents and senior business people.

Helping is the currency here. Members who help rise; members who only take drift down. We keep it small on purpose: a balanced core of 100 to 150 active members, with every application read personally.

And it is run by the people in it, by a council of senior volunteers, not a one-person show.

  • It is not an expensive founder club.
  • It is not a bar meetup full of people trying to sell each other something.
Why now

As AI eats more desk work, a trusted local network of people who actually show up becomes more valuable every year.

A group of senior people each giving a bit of focused time can build something great. Nobody gives up their week; just a little time, pointed in the right direction.

Owen Hughes, founder and chair

House rules

What's shared in Bizcelona stays in Bizcelona.

Seven rules. They are short because we expect you to use judgement.

  1. Give Before You Take

    Share what you know, make introductions and lend a hand before you ask for help yourself. The members who give are the ones who gain most.

  2. Active Participation

    Stay in the conversation. Read the group, reply thoughtfully, and reach out when you spot someone who needs what you know. A passive membership dilutes what we are building.

  3. Mutual Respect and Trust

    Be professional and courteous, keep your commitments and assume good intent. We are building long-term relationships, not transactions.

  4. No Unsolicited Private Messages

    Ask in the group first, or have an existing relationship, before you message someone privately. Cold outreach and sales pitches break trust. Introduce yourself in the group and let it grow from there.

  5. No Promotional Posts

    This is not a marketing channel. No hard sells, no thinly disguised adverts. If your work is relevant to a conversation, say so naturally. Not sure? Ask an admin first. Repeat offenders are removed.

  6. Privacy and Confidentiality

    Do not screenshot, share or republish anything from the group without permission. Treat what members tell you as confidential. Breaches are taken seriously.

  7. Tiered Involvement

    Not everyone is active at the same level, and that is fine. But everyone follows these rules and adds value when they take part. If you are rarely around, ask yourself whether this is the right fit. We are after mutual growth, not passive consumption.

Partnerships

Partners who give before they take.

We want partnerships that make the member experience better, not transactional sponsorships or brand deals.

The right partner shares our commitment to mutual support, real collaboration and long-term community growth. That might be exclusive member benefits, educational opportunities or shared resources. We put quality and relevance ahead of everything else, and we only pursue partnerships that genuinely serve our members.

Venue partners

Our co-working days rotate between partner spaces, and our monthly event needs a good room. If you run a space that suits focused work and lunch together, we would like to hear from you.

Perks and sponsors

If you offer something members would use, such as a perk, a workshop or a resource, tell us what and why. We will say yes only when it is useful.
